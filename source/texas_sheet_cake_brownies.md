# Texas Sheet Cake Brownies

## Ingredients

- 17x11 pan (or 9x13)
- 2 Cups flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 1/4 cup cocoa
- 1 stick (1/2 cup) butter
- 1/2 cup shortening (crisco)
- 1/2 cup buttermilk (or substitute 2 tsp vinegar or lemon juice in 1/2 cup of milk, or use powdered buttermilk)
- 1 cup of strong brewed coffee (or water if you don't want coffee)
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp vanilla

Frosting

- 1 stick (1/2 cup) butter
- 2 tbsp cocoa
- 1/4 cup milk
- 3 1/2 cup unsifted powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Steps

- Preheat oven to 400F
- Combine flour and sugar in large mixing bowl
- In saucepan, combine butter, shortening, coffee, and cocoa, stir and heat to boiling
- Pour boiling coffee mixture over flour/sugar mix
- Add buttermilk, eggs, baking soda, and vanilla
- Mix well - use wodden spoon or mixer on high
- Pour mixture into well buttered pan
- Bake 20 minutes at 400F or until done in center
- While baking, prepare frosting:
- in saucepan, combine butter, cocoa, and milk; heat to boil, stirring constantly
- Mix in powdered sugar and vanilla; stir until combined
- Pour warm mixture over brownies as soon as out of the oven
