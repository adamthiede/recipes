# Mug Brownies

Brownies you can make in a mug in the microwave.

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- 3 tbsp flour
- 1 1/2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp chocolate chips (+ more for topping)
- 1 1/2 tbsp vegetable oil, or coconut oil, or melted butter
- 3 tbsp milk
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- toppings: ice cream, whipped cream, powdered sugar, etc. (optional)

## Steps

- combine dry ingredients, stir
- add wet ingredients and stir until batter-y
- microwave 30 seconds to 1 minute.
